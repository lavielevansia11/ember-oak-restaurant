Our story

One fire. Ten years of tending it.

Ember & Oak started as a farmers' market bread stall in 2015. Today we cook everything over a single oak-fired hearth — the same one that taught us patience, and that bread is worth doing properly.

We work with twelve farms within 100 miles, mill our own grain, and change the menu whenever the season tells us to.

  • 100% oak-fired cooking
  • Farm partnerships within 100 miles
  • House-milled flour daily
